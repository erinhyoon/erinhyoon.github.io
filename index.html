I study how governments and the press define social problems, and how those definitions narrow the policies that follow. My work is comparative and centred on East Asia, with low fertility, gender and labour policy as its main cases.

Current research

In preparation

Is Korea becoming Japan?

Low fertility discourse in newspapers and policy documents, 1995–2020

A bilingual dictionary applied to every mention of low fertility in 27,790 articles from four national dailies, compared with the four Korean Basic Plans and sixteen Japanese White Papers. Korean coverage closely resembles the government's own framing; Japanese coverage does not.

In preparation

Where is low fertility contested?

Framing in the press and in parliament in South Korea and Japan

Newspaper coverage set beside speeches in the National Assembly and the National Diet, measured with a keyword-assisted topic model, to locate whether the division runs between news outlets, between parties, or between the two arenas.
